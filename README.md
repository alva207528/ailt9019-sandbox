### About softball

Softball is a bat-and-ball sport closely related to baseball, but played with a larger and softer ball (typically yellow, around 12 inches / 30 cm in circumference) that is thrown underhand rather than overhand. The standard game has **9 players per team** and is played over **7 innings**, with the goal of scoring more runs than the opponent by hitting the ball and running around four bases.

There are two main variants:

- **Fast-pitch softball** — pitches can reach 60–70+ mph (95–115 km/h); common in international women's competition and the Olympics.
- **Slow-pitch softball** — pitches arc more slowly, popular at recreational and club level, and the version most commonly played in Hong Kong's university leagues.

Softball is the official team sport of many university and corporate leagues in Hong Kong, including mixed-gender formats like the HKUSU Summer League. Core skills include hitting, bunting, base-running, throwing, and fielding — with positions like pitcher, catcher, infielders, and outfielders all requiring different techniques.

Beyond the physical side, softball is a great way to build teamwork, discipline, and friendships — which is why I enjoy playing it.
